⚠️ Safety comes first

HVAC work may involve lethal voltage, pressurized refrigerant, combustion gases, rotating equipment, sharp metal, and high temperatures. Never bypass a safety control. Follow lockout/tagout, manufacturer instructions, EPA rules, and local codes.
